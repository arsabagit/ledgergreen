# How to Install LedgerGreen 📱

LedgerGreen is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**, which means you can install it directly from your browser without visiting an App Store.

## 🍎 For iPhone/iPad (iOS)

1.  Open **Safari** and navigate to:
    [**https://ledgergreen.vercel.app**](https://ledgergreen.vercel.app)
2.  Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom of the screen).
3.  Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (you might need to tap "More..." first).
4.  Enter the name "LedgerGreen" if prompted.
5.  Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.

The app will now appear on your home screen and work just like a native app!

---

## 🤖 For Android

1.  Open **Chrome** and navigate to:
    [**https://ledgergreen.vercel.app**](https://ledgergreen.vercel.app)
2.  Tap the **Menu** icon (three dots ⠇ in the top-right corner).
3.  Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4.  Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm.

---

## 💻 For Desktop (Chrome/Edge)

1.  Navigate to [**https://ledgergreen.vercel.app**](https://ledgergreen.vercel.app).
2.  Look for the install icon (a monitor with a down arrow) in the right side of the address bar.
3.  Click it and select **Install**.
